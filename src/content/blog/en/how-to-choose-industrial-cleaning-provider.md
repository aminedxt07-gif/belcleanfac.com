Choosing the right industrial cleaning company is not a minor decision: a poorly matched provider can mean rushed work, delays, or even damage to your infrastructure. Here are the criteria to check before signing a contract.

## 1. Safety and quality certifications

On an industrial, hospital or logistics site, the teams on the ground need to know the safety rules specific to these environments. **VCA** certification (safety, health and environment on site) is almost a prerequisite for any intervention in an industrial setting in Belgium. Always ask your provider to justify this certification.

## 2. Professional equipment suited to every surface

Cleaning a facade at height, a warehouse floor or an underground car park doesn't call for the same equipment. A serious provider has a varied fleet: boom trucks and scissor lifts for access at height, large-surface scrubbers for industrial floors, reverse-osmosis water systems for streak-free window cleaning, or gentle sandblasting equipment for graffiti removal without damaging surfaces. Missing the right equipment almost always means a lower-quality result or longer timelines.

## 3. The ability to adapt to site constraints

A logistics warehouse running continuously, a hotel with guests on site, or a university campus during term time don't have the same constraints as an empty building. The best cleaning companies know how to organise their interventions in shifted hours, by zone, or outside opening hours so they never disrupt your business.

## 4. Responsiveness on quote requests

A quote that takes weeks to arrive is often a bad sign for the rest of the collaboration. Favour a provider able to assess your needs quickly — ideally within 24 business hours — and to offer a site visit if needed.

## 5. References and geographic coverage

A provider active across several sectors (industry, automotive, hospitality, higher education, commercial) generally has better adaptability than a company specialised in a single type of site. Also check that it can operate across the whole of Belgium if you have multiple locations.

## In summary

VCA certification, varied professional equipment, flexible organisation and commercial responsiveness: these are the four pillars to check before entrusting the upkeep of your site to a provider. At BCF, we've combined these criteria for over 20 years, working with businesses across Belgium.
